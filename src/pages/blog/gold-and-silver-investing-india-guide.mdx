## 3. Statutory Taxation of Precious Metals (FY 2026-27)

<Callout type="tax" title="Current Capital Gains Rates for Gold & Silver">
  1. **Gold & Silver ETFs (Listed Securities):**
     - Short-Term ($\le 12$ months): Taxed at individual slab rate.
     - Long-Term ($> 12$ months): **12.5% without indexation** under Section 112.
  2. **Physical Gold & Silver:**
     - Short-Term ($\le 24$ months): Taxed at individual slab rate.
     - Long-Term ($> 24$ months): **12.5% without indexation** under Section 112.
  3. **Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGB):**
     - Maturity redemption with RBI: **0% Tax (100% Exempt)** under Section 47(viic).
</Callout>
