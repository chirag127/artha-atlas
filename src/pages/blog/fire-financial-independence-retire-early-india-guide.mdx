The global Financial Independence, Retire Early (FIRE) movement relies heavily on the famous 1998 Trinity Study, which popularized the "4% Rule" of thumb. However, copy-pasting American financial assumptions into the Indian macroeconomic environment is an invitation to terminal financial ruin.

---

## 2. Why the American 4% Rule Fails Catastrophically in India

<Callout type="warning" title="The Three Macro Hazards of Indian Early Retirement">
  1. **Persistent High Inflation:** Indian CPI inflation averages 6.0% to 7.0%, while urban healthcare inflation compounds at **12% to 14% annually**.
  2. **Sequence-of-Returns Risk (SRR):** If an early retiree encounters a 2008-style 55% market crash during their first 3 years of retirement while withdrawing living expenses, the portfolio experiences irreversible capital depletion.
  3. **Extended Longevity Runway:** Early retirement at age 35 to 45 implies a multi-decade withdrawal runway of 45 to 55 years.
</Callout>
