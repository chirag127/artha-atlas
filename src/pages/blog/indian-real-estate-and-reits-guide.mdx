Real estate is deeply ingrained in Indian cultural wealth psychology, yet direct physical residential property is frequently one of the lowest-yielding, most illiquid, and legally encumbered asset classes an individual can own. Conversely, the introduction of **Real Estate Investment Trusts (REITs)** and **Small & Medium REITs (SM REITs)** by SEBI has transformed commercial property into a liquid, transparent, and cash-distributing institutional avenue.

## 2. The SEBI REIT Cash-Flow Distribution Waterfall

Under SEBI REIT regulations, listed REITs are legally mandated to distribute at least **90% of Net Distributable Cash Flows (NDCF)** to unitholders.

<Mermaid title="SEBI REIT Cash Flow Distribution & Tax Passthrough Waterfall">

    Tenants["Grade-A Corporate Tenants"] -->|Contractual Monthly Lease Payments| SPVs["Special Purpose Vehicles (SPVs)"]
    SPVs -->|Clean Dividend & Interest Upstreaming| REIT["REIT Trust Level"]
    REIT -->|Mandatory 90% NDCF Distribution| Distribution["Quarterly Unitholder Distribution"]
    Distribution --> Comp1["Dividend Component (Exempt under Sec 10-23FD)"]
    Distribution --> Comp2["Interest Component (Taxable at Slab Rate)"]`}

## 3. Statutory Property Capital Gains Tax (FY 2026-27)

<Callout type="tax" title="Current Real Estate Tax Provisions">
  - **Holding Period Threshold:** 24 months separates Short-Term from Long-Term Capital Gains.
  - **LTCG Rate:** **12.5% without indexation** under Section 112.
  - **Grandfathering Clause:** Resident individuals who acquired property prior to July 23, 2024, can compute tax under BOTH methods (20% with indexation vs 12.5% without) and pay the lower tax amount!
  - **Section 54 Exemption:** Full LTCG exemption by reinvesting capital gains in residential property.
</Callout>
