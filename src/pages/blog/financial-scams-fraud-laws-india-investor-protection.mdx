## 3. Historic Forensic Case Studies

### 1. PACL (Pearls Agrotech Corporation Limited) — ₹49,100 Crores
PACL operated the largest illegal Collective Investment Scheme (CIS) in Indian history, mobilizing over ₹49,100 Crores from 5.8 crore investors. Early unitholders were paid purely from fresh investor subscriptions.

### 2. The Saradha Group Chit Fund Collapse (2013) — ₹2,500 Crores
Operating across West Bengal, Assam, and Odisha, Saradha ran unregulated collective deposit schemes that collapsed when fresh capital inflows halted.
