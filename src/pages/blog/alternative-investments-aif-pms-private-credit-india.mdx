## 3. Statutory Taxation Framework (Section 115UB)

<Callout type="tax" title="Pass-Through Taxation of AIF Category I and II">
  - **Category I & II AIFs:** Granted **statutory pass-through status under Section 115UB**. The fund itself pays zero tax. Income earned is taxed directly in unitholder hands in the same character.
  - **Category III AIFs:** Do NOT enjoy full pass-through status for business income. The fund itself is typically taxed at the Maximum Marginal Rate (MMR) at trust level.
</Callout>
