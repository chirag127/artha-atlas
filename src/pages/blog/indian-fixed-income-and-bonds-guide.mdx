## 4. The Franklin Templeton Debt Crisis (2020 Forensic Analysis)

In April 2020, Franklin Templeton AMC voluntarily wound up six open-ended yield-oriented debt schemes, freezing over ₹25,000 Crores of retail capital.

<Callout type="warning" title="Forensic Breakdown: The 2020 Franklin Templeton Liquidity Freeze">
  - **The Root Cause:** The funds pursued high gross yields by concentrating heavily in illiquid, lower-rated corporate credit papers.
  - **The Catalyst:** Following COVID-19, redemption requests surged. The secondary market for sub-AAA Indian corporate bonds completely evaporated.
  - **The Lesson:** While unitholders eventually recovered over 100% of their principal as underlying bonds matured, the funds suffered a complete 30-month liquidity freeze. In fixed income, **credit risk is liquidity risk in disguise**.
</Callout>
