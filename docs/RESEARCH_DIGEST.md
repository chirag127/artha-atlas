## 1. Statutory Authorities & Regulatory Mandates
1. **Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI)**:
   - Mandate: Protection of investors in securities, regulation and development of securities market.
   - Enforces: SEBI Act 1992, Securities Contracts (Regulation) Act (SCRA) 1956, Depositories Act 1996.
   - Key Regulations: SEBI (Mutual Funds) Regulations 1996, SEBI (Alternative Investment Funds) Regulations 2012, SEBI (Real Estate Investment Trusts) Regulations 2014, SEBI (Infrastructure Investment Trusts) Regulations 2014, SEBI (Portfolio Managers) Regulations 2020, SEBI (Research Analysts) Regulations 2014.
   - Grievance Portal: SEBI SCORES 2.0 (`scores.sebi.gov.in`) and SMART ODR (`smartodr.in`).

2. **Reserve Bank of India (RBI)**:
   - Mandate: Monetary stability, currency management, banking supervision, regulation of non-banking financial companies (NBFCs), foreign exchange (FEMA 1999), and government bond retail access (RBI Retail Direct).
   - Key Directions: Master Direction – Non-Banking Financial Company – Peer to Peer Lending Platform (Reserve Bank) Directions, Master Direction on Non-Banking Financial Companies, RBI Retail Direct Scheme (G-Secs, T-Bills, SDLs).
   - Grievance Portal: RBI Complaint Management System (CMS) (`cms.rbi.org.in`) and Sachet (`sachet.rbi.org.in`) for unregistered deposit entities.

3. **Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority (PFRDA)**:
   - Mandate: Promotion and regulation of National Pension System (NPS) and Atal Pension Yojana (APY).
   - Key Structures: Point of Presence (PoP), Central Recordkeeping Agencies (CRAs: Protean, KFintech, CAMS), Pension Fund Managers (PFMs), and NPS Trust.

4. **Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (IRDAI)**:
   - Mandate: Protection of policyholders' interests, regulation of life and general insurers, health insurance, and insurance intermediaries. Bima Bharosa grievance portal.

## 3. Fraud Prevention, Banned Schemes & Case Studies
- **Banning of Unregulated Deposit Schemes (BUDS) Act, 2019**: Complete prohibition of unregulated deposit-taking activities, Ponzi operations, and unregistered collective investment schemes.
- **Key Historic Case Studies Covered**:
  1. *Saradha & Rose Valley*: Ponzi chit fund collapse in West Bengal and Odisha.
  2. *PACL (Pearls)*: Massive collective investment scheme disguised as agriculture land development.
  3. *Sahara India Real Estate*: Optionally Fully Convertible Debentures (OFCDs) issued without SEBI prospectus.
  4. *Franklin Templeton 6 Debt Schemes Wind-up (2020)*: Illiquid lower-rated credit, secondary market freeze, side-pocketing, segregation, and voting procedures.
  5. *IL&FS & DHFL defaults (2018)*: Commercial paper contagion, rating agency failures, and NBFC asset-liability mismatch (ALM).
